Help center

Quick guidance for common account and meal-management questions.

How do I update my account name?

Change the display name in Account details above and choose Save account details. Your email and role cannot be edited here.

Where can I find my meals and meal history?

Students can open the dashboard to see today's meals, the weekly menu, meal history and recorded expenses. Ask the mess administrator if a record needs correction.

How do I update the menu or review records?

Use the Admin tools shortcuts above or the navigation links at the top of the page.

Where do I get more help?

Contact your mess administrator for sign-in assistance, account access, meal-record corrections or questions about how your institution manages this portal.

Terms & conditions

This summary is an application notice, not legal advice. The institution operating the portal should review and replace it with its approved terms before production use.

  1. Use of the portal. Use your own account and provide accurate information. Do not attempt to access another person's account or change records you are not authorized to manage.
  2. Meal information. Menus, meal choices and expense records are provided for mess administration. Confirm billing or disputed records with the institution responsible for the mess.
  3. Availability. The portal is provided by the institution operating it and may be changed or unavailable during maintenance.
  4. Account responsibility. Keep your sign-in details private and notify the administrator if you believe your account has been accessed improperly.

Privacy policy

This is a starter notice. The institution operating the portal must confirm its retention, access and contact details before using it as a formal privacy policy.

  1. Information used. The application stores account name, email address and role, along with meal choices, menu information and meal-record or expense details needed to operate the mess portal.
  2. Purpose and access. Information is used to provide account access and manage mess operations. Administrators may access student and meal records for those purposes.
  3. Storage and security. Account and operational data is stored in the MySQL database configured by the institution. The current application version stores passwords as plain text; it is not suitable for production use with real credentials until password hashing and related security controls are implemented. Do not reuse a sensitive password on this system.
  4. Questions or requests. Contact the institution or mess administrator to ask about access, correction or deletion of account and meal information.
